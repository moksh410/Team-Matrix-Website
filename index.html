$ cat matrix.txt

                
Born from late-night security talk

                
Team Matrix was founded by a group of security enthusiasts at SCIT, Pune — born from late-night
                  discussions and a shared passion for cybersecurity.

                
Guided by the values of communities like ISACA and partnered with Null, the open security community,
                  we focus on security awareness, hands-on research and a centralized knowledge platform for
                  professionals and enthusiasts alike.

                
Recognized as MCCIA's Cybersecurity Club of the Month (January 2025), we keep
                  working toward a secure, resilient digital future.

                cd /about-us →
              

              
            

          

          

            

              

                
$ cat isaca.txt

                
Global standards. Local chapter energy.

                
Founded in 1969, ISACA is a global association for IT governance, risk and cybersecurity with
                  140,000+ members worldwide — home to certifications like CISA, CISM and CRISC.

                
Through its chapters and events, ISACA fosters a community of innovation, networking and knowledge
                  sharing — values that shape how Team Matrix operates every day.

                cd /about-us →
              

              
            

          

          

            

              

                
$ cat null.txt

                
India's largest open security community

                
Founded in 2007, Null exists to spread information-security awareness and give hackers and defenders
                  a collaborative place to learn, share and grow — through meetups, workshops and CTFs across India.

                
Our collaboration with Null plugs Team Matrix straight into the country's most influential security
                  community.
