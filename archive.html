CYBERON 2024 — "Ethos and Echoes"

CyberON 2024 was built around the theme "Ethos and Echoes: Securing the Intersection of Humanity and Technology" — raising awareness about ethical responsibility in the digital age and securing human interactions in a tech-driven world.

Speakers — the panel on "Human-Centric Cybersecurity: The Last Line of Defence" featured Advocate Rajas Pingle, Pankaj Chanekar, Rohitha Chowdary, Rushikesh Nandedkar and Aalok Karnik, moderated by Sanyogeeta Rananaware.

Competitions —

  • Capture the Flag — high-energy puzzle-solving against the clock.
  • Guardian of Cyber Galaxy — maze navigation, phishing detection, DDoS defense, final clue decoding.
  • Hackscape — a team-based escape room of escalating security puzzles.
  • Emocrypt — a campus-wide treasure hunt of riddles and emoji-based clues.

DATE: 25 FEB 2025LOC: PCMC COLLEGE, PUNESTATUS: COMPLETE ✓
