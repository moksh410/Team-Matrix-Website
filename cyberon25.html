THREADS OF TRUST
The “threads of trust” are the interconnected layers or dimensions of trust that together create a secure environment. Think of them like woven threads in a strong fabric

if one weakens, the whole fabric is at risk.
Capture The Flag
Everything you need to know about CTF – Capture the Flag The Capture-the-Flag (CTF) competition is a high energy cybersecurity event that pushes your technical, analytical, and problem-solving skills to the limit. Participants will dive into carefully designed puzzles, forensic files and cryptography challenges all while racing against the clock.
Every solved puzzle reveals a hidden “flag”, and each flag brings you one step closer to victory.
In this battle of wits, it’s not just about what you know, but how quickly and creatively you can apply it. CTF is designed to test your presence of mind, logical reasoning, and technical instinct under pressure. Are you ready to think like an attacker, defend like a pro, and uncover the secrets hidden in the challenges?
7th Sense
In a world driven by split-second decisions, your team must rely on sharp instincts, coordination, and trust to navigate through a series of challenges. Time is limited, distractions are everywhere, and every moment counts.
Only those who master speed, communication, and teamwork can rise above the chaos and claim victory. In this action-packed event, you’ll be tested on how fast you think, how well you work together, and how much you trust each other.
Every second saved or wasted could determine whether your team crosses the finish line first or falls behind. Are you ready to unlock your seventh sense and win the challenge?
MazeStech
The digital world is like a giant maze filled with surprises, shortcuts, and hidden traps. In Mazestech, your team must find its way through this maze by solving puzzles, making quick decisions, and overcoming obstacles that test your presence of mind. Every round brings a new twist from funny and fast-paced challenges to tricky situations that demand teamwork and strategy.
Each correct move takes you closer to victory, while every mistake could set you back. Only the sharpest, fastest, and most united team will conquer the maze and claim the title of champions.
Data Heist
Hackers have stolen sensitive data and locked it inside a secret Vault. Your mission is to track down hidden clues, solve cyber crossword, and assemble the final password before your rivals do. Every second counts – hesitation could cost your team the prize.
This event blends cyber knowledge, puzzle-solving, and strategy with the thrill of a live chase. Each round raises the intensity, leading up to the ultimate climax: unlocking the Vault.
