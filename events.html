CYBERON // flagship fest

$ man cyberon

Held annually in October, Cyberon is the flagship event of Team Matrix, designed to spread cybersecurity awareness through an exciting mix of activities. Whether you're a tech enthusiast or just someone keen on digital safety, Cyberon has something for everyone.

  • Fun and Engaging Non-Tech Games – Making cybersecurity education accessible and enjoyable.
  • Technical Capture The Flag (CTF) Challenges – Testing and enhancing cybersecurity skills through hands-on problem-solving.
  • Expert Panel Discussions – Industry professionals from various domains share their insights on cybersecurity trends, challenges, and best practices.
  • Sponsorships – Supported by leading companies and organizations, Cyberon provides an excellent platform for industry collaboration and networking opportunities.
  • Food Stalls – A variety of food options to keep participants energized and make the event an enjoyable social experience.

Cyberon is the perfect blend of learning and entertainment, ensuring that cybersecurity awareness reaches a wide audience in an interactive way.

[ op 02 / january ]

DATA PRIVACY WEEK // dpw

$ man dpw

With an increasing focus on data protection, Data Privacy Week serves as an annual initiative to educate individuals and organizations about the importance of data privacy. Each year, DPW explores a unique theme, delving into critical aspects of data protection.

Under DPW, we hosted Privathon, our interactive cybersecurity festival that tested privacy, ethics, and responsibility.

$ open logs/privathon_2026.log

PRIVATHON 2026 — "Privacy Spectrum"

Privacy Spectrum explored data privacy beyond simple binaries, covering personal protection, cybersecurity, legal rights, and ethics. This event revealed how these interconnected dimensions shape privacy for individuals and society in the digital age.

Events —

  • Level Up — Campus-wide interactive challenge. Chase clues, crack puzzles, clear levels, and prove your team has what it takes to win.
  • Faisla Unlocked — A courtroom-style showdown. Take the stand as Prosecutor or Defence, debate real-world data privacy cases, and prove your mastery of privacy, ethics, and responsibility.

[ op 03 / monthly outreach ]

CYBER JAAGRUKTA DIWAS // cjd

$ man cjd

Cyber Jagrukta Diwas (CJD) is a unique, ongoing initiative led by Team Matrix to promote cybersecurity awareness across society. Held monthly, this outreach program reflects our commitment to a digitally safe environment through continuous education and engagement.

Members voluntarily visit government schools, underprivileged neighborhoods, IT companies, college staff and high-school students — tailoring every session to its audience, making complex cyber concepts simple and relatable.

CJD is not just an event — it's a movement towards a safer cyberspace, driven by the passion and responsibility of young cyber advocates.
